DESIGN DIRECTION — VERSION 1.8

The redesign follows patterns commonly used by leading luxury hotel brands:
- Image-first accommodation storytelling rather than small generic cards.
- Restrained editorial typography, generous whitespace and a warm material palette.
- Clear room facts such as size, occupancy, bed type, view and rate.
- Accommodation category filtering for easier comparison.
- Strong distinction between commercial room listings and experiential resort facilities.
- Facilities presented as immersive destination stories with full-bleed photography.
- Emphasis on local character, natural setting and indoor-outdoor living.

The implementation uses only the images already supplied with the Silver Rock website package.
