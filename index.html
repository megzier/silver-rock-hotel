Welcome to Malindi

Silver Rock Boutique Resort by MOKAWA

A relaxed beachfront retreat where Malindi's coastal character, warm Swahili hospitality and thoughtful modern comfort come together.

Managed by MOKAWA Group and recognised with the Best-in-Class Heritage Hospitality Award 2024, Silver Rock offers an inviting base for quiet escapes, family holidays, celebrations and business retreats beside the Indian Ocean.

From light-filled rooms and beachfront residences to tropical pools, coastal dining and a boutique in-house spa, every part of the resort is designed to help guests slow down and feel genuinely cared for.

Dining

Inspired by fresh coastal catch and Swahili spice traditions, Silver Rock's dining venues bring together beachfront ambience and memorable flavour — from relaxed lunches to fine evening dining under the stars.

Baharini Restaurant

Baharini Restaurant brings together the best of Swahili coastal cuisine and international favourites, served on an open-air terrace with unobstructed views of the Indian Ocean. Fresh catch of the day, char-grilled seafood platters and fragrant coconut curries headline a menu built around local spice traditions.

Whether you're joining us for a leisurely breakfast, a relaxed lunch, or a candlelit dinner beneath the stars, Baharini is designed for guests who want their meal to be as memorable as the view.

Boutique Spa
